# ScholiPhi assessment review

## Register

product

## Source of authority

The user requested alignment with the recent Downloads references, including `ScholiPhi — Test Report UI (Teacher & Student views).html` and its eight-page PDF, and the named teacher-analysis, class-matrix, answer-review, and individual-report PDFs. The duplicated individual-report PDF is the same source. This records that supplied baseline, rather than proposing a new design direction.

## Users and purpose

Teachers upload a paper, correct extracted questions and tags, approve answers and marks, choose evaluation rules, review marked scripts and release results. Teachers use class analysis and the matrix to plan follow-up. Students and parents read released individual results and plain-language guidance. Teachers compare scans and feedback on desktop; parents need the same information to read comfortably on a phone.

## Voice and visual character

The reference is calm, precise and school-oriented: ScholiPhi branding, navy headings, a light lavender canvas, compact tables, and clear positive/partial/missing states. Explain what the marks mean and the next action in ordinary language. The documented reference, rather than a generic administration dashboard, is the visual standard.

## Design principles

- The original paper sits beside the question or feedback being reviewed, with reciprocal selection.
- Keep the teacher's three decisions visible: check questions, approve marking, approve release.
- Work through one answer at a time while retaining question navigation and mark totals.
- Show actual student data and distinguish suggestions, saved drafts, and completed actions.
- Make the route from class overview to an individual answer and back obvious.
- Retain a useful empty state when history, blueprint, attendance, or assignment integrations are absent.

## Anti-references

Avoid exposing provider configuration, raw JSON, internal routing codes or R1/R2 labels in teacher and parent journeys. Avoid alert-only actions, fabricated student histories or school activities, and claims that suggestions were sent or assigned when no action took place.

## Accessibility and inclusion

Preserve reference information and hierarchy while providing labelled inputs, keyboard navigation, visible focus, status text alongside colour, readable mobile text, local table scrolling, and reduced-motion support. The references prescribe no additional institutional accessibility standard.
